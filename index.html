Questions parents and players ask

Is Matri Rush good for kids?

Yes. Matri Rush is family-friendly, with no violence and calm, colourblind-friendly visuals, so players of any age can enjoy it. It is a gentle way for younger players to practise focus and spatial reasoning. It does include optional in-app purchases, so we suggest turning on your device's purchase controls for younger players.

What skills does Matri Rush help build?

Every level is a timed tiling puzzle that exercises spatial reasoning, planning, pattern recognition and quick decision-making. The difficulty rises gradually across 6,000 levels, so the challenge keeps pace with the player.

Is Matri Rush free?

Yes. Matri Rush is free to download and play on the App Store and Google Play. It offers optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch.

What kind of game is Matri Rush?

It is a fast, timed brick-tiling puzzle. You snap coloured bricks onto a board to fill every gap before the timer runs out, across 112 themed villages and 6,000 levels.

What else are you making?

Two more titles, both in development and both below on this page. Rush Delivery 3D is a colour-match traffic-jam puzzle, and TicTacTalk teaches a language through tic-tac-toe. Same desk, same engine work, same habit of shipping the mechanic before the marketing.

Act II Rush Delivery 3D Traffic-jam puzzle · In development

Clear the jam.
Make the drop.

A queue of hungry customers waits at the shop door. Below them, the lot is a knot of vans, buses and scooters, and every one of them can only reverse the way its own arrow points.

  1. Read the knotEvery vehicle has one direction. Tap it and it goes, if nothing is in the way.
  2. Fill the baysWhatever escapes parks in a bay and loads the customers matching its colour.
  3. Only the front boardsThe queue loads strictly from the front, so the wrong colour in a bay is a bay you have lost.
  4. Break your own ruleThe heli airlifts a customer out of the middle of the queue. It is the one thing on the board that ignores the front-of-line rule.

Act III TicTacTalk Language app · In development

Get it right,
get the square.

A language app that is actually a game of tic-tac-toe. You read a short everyday scene, then answer a question about it. Right answer, your mark goes down. Wrong answer, the bot takes the square instead.

  1. Scenes, not flashcardsMeeting a neighbour, asking for directions, ordering a coffee. Real sentences you would actually say.
  2. Comprehension is the moveThe board only advances when you understood the line, so guessing costs you the square.
  3. Two languages at oncePick the language you are learning and the language you are comfortable in. Any pairing works.

Three titles, one desk in Ikeja.

Independent and self-published, out of Ikeja in Lagos. No publisher, no live-ops department, no growth team. Three titles now, and all three lean on the same engine work: a generator, a solver, and the habit of shipping the mechanic before the marketing.

Every Matri Rush level is machine-checked before it ships: one solution, reachable without guessing. Every Rush Delivery jam is cleared by a search first. A board that rewards guessing under a timer is the one thing neither generator is allowed to produce.

Questions, bug reports, or a level that felt unfair go to support@matrirush.com. We read all of it.
