Straight answers

Before you tap install

Where can I download Matri Rush?

It is out now on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, free to download. The Android build is finished but not listed on Google Play yet, so there is no Play link to give you today. This page gets one the moment there is.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. No violence, calm colourblind-friendly visuals, and one-finger controls, so any age can play. It does include optional in-app purchases, so turn on your device's purchase controls for younger players.

Is it free?

Yes, free to download and play. There are optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch. Nothing is behind a paywall.

Rush Delivery 3D · free on the App Store

Clear the jam. Make the drop.

Customers queue at the shop door. Below them the lot is a knot of vans, buses and scooters, and each one can only pull out the way its arrow points.

  1. Read the knotTap a vehicle and it goes, if nothing is in its way.
  2. Only the front boardsThe queue loads strictly from the front, so a wrong colour in a bay is a bay you have lost.
  3. Break your own ruleThe heli airlifts someone out of the middle of the queue. It is the one thing on the board that ignores the front-of-line rule.

Bottle Rush Shooting 3D · free on the App Store

Line up the shot. Keep the chain alive.

Cartoon bottles arc across a fairground sky and you shoot them out of it. Break them back to back and the multiplier climbs — but some of them are packed with explosives, and you only ever get three lives.

  1. Tap or hold to fireThe reticle follows your finger, and a miss costs you nothing at all.
  2. The chain is the scoreBack-to-back breaks build the combo up to ×10. A miss will not break it — only the combo timer running out.
  3. Know what not to shootHit a bomb, or let a good bottle fall past you, and that is one of the three gone.

TicTacTalk · in development

Get it right, get the square.

A language app that is actually a game of tic-tac-toe. Read a short everyday scene, answer a question about it. Right answer, your mark goes down. Wrong answer, the bot takes the square.

  1. Scenes, not flashcardsMeeting a neighbour, asking for directions, ordering a coffee.
  2. Comprehension is the moveThe board only advances when you understood the line, so guessing costs you the square.

BAWA · in development

Find a hand, close by.

The one thing here that is not a game. BAWA puts skilled workers and the people who need them on the same map, and handles the awkward part in the middle: agreeing the job, and getting paid for it.

  1. Two apps in oneHiring side and working side, in the same install. Pick a side and the whole app changes colour with you: blue to hire, red to work.
  2. Near you, not nationwideSearch is built around where you actually are, so the plumber you find is one who can turn up today.
  3. Paid in the appCard payments in, payouts out, so nobody has to argue about a transfer screenshot.
