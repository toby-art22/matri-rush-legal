TicTacTalk

Get it right, get the square.

A language app that is actually a game of tic-tac-toe. Read a short everyday scene, answer a question about it. Right answer, your mark goes down. Wrong answer, the bot takes the square.

Questions

Where it stands

Can I download TicTacTalk yet?

Not yet. It is still in development and is not listed on the App Store or Google Play. This page is where the release will be announced, and support@matrirush.com will tell you when it lands if you ask.

Which languages will it teach?

English, French, Turkish, Japanese, Yorùbá and Spanish at the start.

How does it work?

You read a short everyday scene and answer a question about it. A right answer puts your mark on the tic-tac-toe board; a wrong one hands the square to the bot.
