BAWA

Find a hand, close by.

The one thing on this shelf that is not a game. BAWA puts skilled workers and the people who need them on the same map, and handles the awkward part in the middle: agreeing the job, and getting paid for it.

Coming soon to theApp Store Coming soon toGoogle Play
Built for Nigeria first Not released yet

How it will work

Two apps, one install.

  1. Two sides in oneA hiring side and a working side. Pick a side and the whole app changes colour with you: blue to hire, red to work.
  2. Near you, not nationwideSearch is built around where you actually are, so the plumber you find is one who can turn up today.
  3. Paid in the appCard payments in, payouts out, so nobody has to argue about a transfer screenshot.
