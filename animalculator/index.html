Questions

Before you download

Is Animalculator free?

The app is free, and the birds, house cats and panda packs are free with it. One optional payment opens the rest of the menagerie forever. There is no subscription.

Does it show ads?

No. No adverts of any kind, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising identifier.

Does it need an account or a connection?

Neither. There is no sign-in, and the whole menagerie ships inside the app, so it works with no connection at all.

How many animals are there?

199 illustrations across 22 packs, plus one animal a day with a fact about it.

Support
