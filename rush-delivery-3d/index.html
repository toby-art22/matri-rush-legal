How it plays

Read the knot. Free the right one.

  2. Only the front boardsThe queue loads strictly from the front, so a wrong colour in a bay is a bay you have lost.

Questions

Before you download

Is Rush Delivery 3D free?

Yes — free to download and play on iPhone and iPad, with optional in-app purchases. Nothing in the game is locked behind a payment.

How do you play it?

Tap a vehicle and it drives the way its arrow points, if nothing is in its way. The customer queue loads strictly from the front, so a vehicle in the wrong colour bay is a bay you have lost. The helicopter is the one thing that ignores the front-of-line rule — it airlifts someone out of the middle of the queue.

How many levels are there?

9,000, and they open one rule at a time rather than throwing every mechanic at you in the first hour.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. No violence, no chat, one-finger controls and colour-coded vehicles. It does include optional in-app purchases, so turn on your device's purchase controls for younger players.

Is it on Android?

Not on Google Play yet. Today the App Store is the only place to get it.
