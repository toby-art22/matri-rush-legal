How it plays

Tap to fire. The chain is the score.

  1. Tap or hold to fireThe reticle follows your finger, and a miss costs you nothing at all.
  2. The chain is the scoreBack-to-back breaks build the combo up to ×10. Only the combo timer running out ends it.
  3. Know what not to shootHit a bomb, or let a good bottle fall past you, and that is one of the three lives gone.
  4. Spend the runScore turns into coins, and coins buy blasters, finishes and retry gems.

Questions

Before you download

Is Bottle Rush Shooting 3D free?

Yes — free to download and play on iPhone and iPad. The shop sells optional coin and gem bundles, and two places offer you a rewarded video if you want one. Every coin can also be earned by playing, and a full run never requires a payment.

How does the combo multiplier work?

Break bottles back to back and the multiplier climbs to ×10. A miss does not break the chain — only the combo timer running out, shooting a bomb, or letting a good bottle fall past you.

What are retry gems?

A gem lets you carry on after a game over. Starting a fresh run from the home screen is always free. You get one gem in the free daily crate every 24 hours, and coins buy more in the shop.

Is it safe for kids?

There is no chat, no accounts, no links out to social networks, and nothing collected about the player. The action is cartoon target-shooting at bottles with a toy blaster — no characters, no blood.

It does have optional rewarded ads and optional in-app purchases. Neither ever happens without a deliberate tap, and both can be switched off with Screen Time on iOS or Family Link on Android.

Is it on Android?

The Android build exists but is not listed on Google Play yet, so today the App Store is the only place to get it.

Support

Bug reports, a device the game runs badly on, or a question about a purchase all go to support@matrirush.com. A real person reads every message, usually replying within two working days.
