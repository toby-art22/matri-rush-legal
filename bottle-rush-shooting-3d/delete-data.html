Deleting your data

Bottle Rush Shooting 3D  ·  Account & data deletion

There is no account to delete.

Bottle Rush Shooting 3D has no sign-in and no server. We hold no data about you at all, so there is nothing on our side to request the deletion of. The only data the Game creates is a save file on your own device, and you can erase it yourself in seconds.

What exists, and where

One record, in the Game's own local storage on your device, under the key bottleRushShooting3D.save.v1. It holds your coins, retry gems, best score, top five runs, the guns and finishes you own, your runs-played count, your daily crate timestamp, your profile name, chosen character and “Anonymous” setting, and your sound and vibration preferences. It contains no personal identifiers, and it has never been transmitted anywhere.

Option 1 — Reset progress inside the Game

  1. Open Bottle Rush Shooting 3D.
  2. Tap SETTINGS on the home screen.
  3. Tap RESET PROGRESS.
  4. Tap it again when it reads TAP AGAIN TO ERASE to confirm.

Everything above is wiped immediately and the Game starts over from a fresh save. This cannot be undone, and we cannot restore it — there is no backup anywhere.

Option 2 — Clear the app's storage

Android: Settings › Apps › Bottle Rush Shooting 3D › Storage › Clear storage (or Clear data).

iOS: iOS has no per-app data clear, so use Option 1 or Option 3.

Option 3 — Uninstall the Game

Deleting the app removes its storage with it, on both Android and iOS. Nothing of yours remains, on the device or anywhere else.

Retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Once you have used any of the options above, the data is gone at the moment you act — there is no grace period, no archive and no backup copy held by us or by any third party.

Still want it in writing?

Email support@matrirush.com and we will confirm within 30 days that Matri Rush holds no personal data relating to you in connection with Bottle Rush Shooting 3D.
